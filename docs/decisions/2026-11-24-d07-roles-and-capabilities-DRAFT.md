## Context
The register keeps roles admin-maintained and refuses to infer authority from a title.

## Options
1. **Keep the default in force (recommended):** admin-maintained attributes; no authority inferred from a title.
2. Infer capabilities from job title — simpler to explain, but bypasses the admin gate.
3. Self-declared roles with admin veto — more flexible, shifts the control point.

## Consequences of the default
Unchanged from the register default; the policy engine remains the only authority source.
