## Decision process

1. Owner picks a short list to test for pronunciation, recall and buyer understanding.
2. Qualified counsel searches the current USPTO trademark-search system and relevant jurisdictions,

3. Check domain and organization-handle availability directly; retain dated evidence.
4. Owner records the chosen name and clearance limitations before external branding changes.
5. Keep repository identifiers unchanged until a separately scoped rename is approved.
